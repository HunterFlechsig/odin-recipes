Chorizo Breakfast Burritos

Home

Description

Delicous brekfast burritos. Great for anytime: breakfast, lunch, or dinner!

Ingredients

  • Cooking Oil
  • 5 Large Tortilla (12")
  • 1lb Chorizo (any kind)
  • 2 cups Frozen hashbrowns
  • 1 cup shredded cheese
  • 10 learge eggs

Steps

  1. Coat frying pan with oil. Cook chorizo over medium heat until brown. Remove from pan
  2. Beat eggs and scramble on medium-low heat. Salt and pepper to taste. Remove when done
  3. Cook hasbrowns until no loner frozen and crispy.
  4. Combine eggs, chorizo, and hashbrowns and mix together.
  5. Warm Tortillas in microwave fro 30 seconds. Scoop a good portion of chorizo-egg mix into middle of each tortila and top with shredded chees. Roll up like a burrito.
