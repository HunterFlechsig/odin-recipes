Chicken Pesto Pasta

Home

Description

This chicken pesto pasta is easy and delicious. Good for meal prep. Enjoy!

Ingredients

  • Cooking Oil
  • 1-2lbs chicken breast, cut into bite sized pieces
  • 1/2 cup pesto suace
  • Salt, pepper, garlic powder, italian seasoning
  • 1 package of a pasta of your choosing

Steps

  1. Bring a large palt of salted water to a boil. Add pasta and cook for 8-10 mintues; drain.
  2. Season chicken with salt, pepper, garlic powder, italian seasoning. Add to heated skillet coated in oil. Cook until chicken is golde nand cooked through
  3. Combine pasta, chicken and pesto in a large bowl. Toss to combine evenly.
