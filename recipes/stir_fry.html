Easy Chicken Stir Fry

Description

This chicken chicken stir fry is easy and delicious. Good for meal prep. Enjoy!

Ingredients

  • Large bag of frozen stir fry vegtables with sauce
  • 2-3lbs of chicken breast
  • Cooking Oil
  • Salt, pepper, garlic powder

Steps

  1. Cut chicken into bit size chunks
  2. Season chicken with salt, pepper, and garlic powder
  3. Coat frying pan with oil. Cook chicken over medium heat until cooked through. Set chicken aside.
  4. Cook vegtables as described on bag. Near the end, add chicken and suace in and stir together
